Our Story

Who We Are

Lanka Travellers is an accredited Sri Lankan travel agency founded with a passion to show travelers the beauty, rich culture, and natural biodiversity of Sri Lanka.

Over the past decade, we have helped thousands of international travelers and families experience the authentic sights, sounds, and tastes of our island. From royal ancient kingdoms to tranquil hill retreats and southern coastal beaches, our dedicated team takes care of every detail.

We are fully registered with the Sri Lanka Tourism Development Authority (SLTDA) and work closely with verified local drivers, hotels, and guides to ensure high quality and safety at all times.

Our Vision

To become the most reliable and trusted travel agency in Sri Lanka, recognized for promoting authentic culture, sustainable tourism, and unforgettable travel memories for every guest.

Our Mission

To provide personalized, safe, and affordable travel itineraries with friendly local chauffeur guides, high standards of hospitality, and honest transparent pricing.

What We Do

Our Services Offered

Comprehensive travel solutions for all types of visitors across Sri Lanka.

1. Tour Packages

Custom multi-day tour packages covering cultural heritage sites, hill country, and beach getaways.

2. Chauffeur Guide Services

Private cars and passenger vans driven by government-licensed, English-speaking driver guides.

3. Hotel Bookings

Reservations at verified hotels, eco-lodges, tea bungalows, and luxury beach resorts.

4. Safari & Excursions

4x4 wildlife jeep safaris in Yala and Minneriya, whale watching trips, and mountain scenic train tickets.

5. Airport Transfers

Prompt 24/7 airport pick-up and drop-off service at Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB).

6. Group & Family Travel

Special arrangements and discounts for families, corporate retreats, and school tour groups.
